PEACE OF MIND, WITH BOOTS ON THE GROUND

Mountain homes need someone who is actually nearby.

When you are away from your Evergreen-area property, small issues can turn into expensive ones. A drop in temperature, a heat problem, a water leak, a storm, a guest issue or a missed turnover can become a headache very quickly. The goal is simple: be your dependable local set of eyes, help document what is happening at the property, and take care of the practical details that are difficult to manage from a distance.

TWO SIMPLE SERVICES

Choose the service that fits your property.

No long list of confusing packages. The business is built around two core services, with additional help available when you need it.

TIER 1
⌂

Mountain Home Watch

For second homes, vacant homes and properties that need regular local attention while you are away.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly visits with a consistent 25-point visual inspection.
  • Check for freeze and water risk, heating issues, visible plumbing problems and unusual conditions.
  • Inspect key areas for storm damage, roof or ice-dam concerns, pests and wildlife intrusion.
  • Review accessible interior and exterior areas and photograph anything that needs attention.
  • Receive a digital visit report with date-stamped photos after each scheduled inspection.
  • Priority winter response when severe cold or major weather creates a concern at the property.

STR Turnover & Host Care

For Airbnb, VRBO and other short-term-rental owners who want one reliable local contact between guests.

WHY LOCAL MATTERS

Mountain properties are different.

Owning a foothills home from Denver—or from out of state—can mean dealing with weather, access and maintenance from hundreds of miles away. A local contact can respond to the things that simply cannot wait for your next trip up.

01

Based in the mountains

We are local to the Evergreen / 80439 area and familiar with the realities of steep driveways, mountain weather, changing conditions and properties spread across the foothills.

02

Documented, not vague

Instead of a quick “everything looks good” text, scheduled Home Watch visits can include a consistent checklist, notes and date-stamped photos so you can see what was actually checked.

03

One local point of contact

When something falls outside normal cleaning or home-watch duties, we can help communicate with the appropriate local professional rather than leaving you to start from scratch.

04

A network beyond our scope

For larger repairs or specialized work, we intend to build relationships with reputable local contractors and service providers for plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, snow, carpentry and other trades.

FAQ

A few things property owners usually want to know.

How often can you check my home?

Home Watch is designed around weekly or bi-weekly visits. A different schedule can be discussed depending on the property and your needs.

Do you provide cleaning?

Yes. Turnover cleaning can be handled directly or coordinated with a local cleaning partner, depending on scheduling and the property.

What happens if you find a problem?

You receive the information and photos from the visit. When appropriate, we can help coordinate access and connect you with the right local contractor or service provider.

Do you do major repairs yourselves?

No. The service is focused on Home Watch, turnover and local coordination. Larger or specialized work can be referred to or coordinated with qualified local professionals.

Can you respond during severe winter weather?

We can offer priority winter response for clients when severe weather creates a reasonable concern at the property, subject to road conditions and safe access.

What areas do you serve?

The core service area is Evergreen and nearby foothills communities. Exact service boundaries can be discussed based on the property's location and access.
