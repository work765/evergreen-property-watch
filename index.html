FAQ

A few things people usually ask.

Can I just book one small job?

Yes. Salting the steps, dropping off firewood or checking on the house after a storm are all fine as one-time jobs. Regular visits are there if you want them.

What do I get after a visit?

Date-stamped photos and a short note after regular visits. Full inspections include a written PDF report you can keep or share.

What if the job is too big for you?

I'll tell you honestly. I handle small repairs myself, and for larger or specialized work I'll help you get the right professional involved.

What areas do you serve?

Evergreen and nearby foothills communities. If you're not sure whether your property is in range, just ask.
